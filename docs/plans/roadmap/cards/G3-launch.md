# G3 · Launch

**Bucket** G Going public · **Part two** · **Status: planned** · **Waits for** G1, R3, P1 (and R1, R2 through them) · **Runs alongside** nothing · **Feeds** P2, P3 (testers come from users)

## Brief

The consultant's advice: launch once, after notarization and iCloud Drive ([memo](../strategy-memo.md) §5, item 7). Post to r/macapps and Show HN, and reply in the OneDrive-icon threads people already wrote. A comparable app, Crisp, built on the same stack, reached about 2.4k GitHub stars in under four months [S]. Launch is also where P2 and P3's testers come from: Dropbox and Google Drive users who answer the post.

Posting is outward-facing and in your name, so **you post everything**. This card prepares the drafts and checks that everything a newcomer clicks works.

Read the [campaign rules](../README.md#rules-every-card-follows) and G1's hand-off.

**The idea:** an opening night. Rehearse until the doors work, then open them once.

## Work

- **Drafts,** kept outside the repo in `~/Local Documents/Velum/launch/`:
  - an r/macapps post;
  - a Show HN title, post and first comment ("how it knows without permissions" is the interesting part);
  - a short reply for each OneDrive-icon thread.

  Check each thread first: still open, still relevant, still allowing replies.
- **A launch checklist,** run the day before, signed out:
  - the DMG downloads and opens without "Open Anyway";
  - `brew install --cask` works;
  - Sparkle's feed is live;
  - issue templates and Sponsors work;
  - the README's images load.
- **The first week:** a short plan for triaging issues. Answer provider requests with G2's guide, and ask willing Dropbox and Google Drive users to be P2's or P3's tester.

## Done when

- The checklist passes.
- The drafts are ready and you have read them.
- You have posted, on your own.

## Ownership and limits

You own the drafts and the checklist. Never post, reply, message or vote on the user's behalf.
