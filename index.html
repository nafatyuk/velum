Velum

A macOS menu bar app, in development by one person.

A velum is a thin veil of cloud that stretches across several separate clouds and joins them.

Velum shows every cloud storage account on a Mac (OneDrive, iCloud Drive and Google Drive) under one menu bar icon: whether each account is in sync, what's still uploading, and recent activity. It reads what the sync apps already keep on the Mac and makes no network connections of its own.

What it does with Google

One optional feature, off until the user turns it on for an account, signs in to Google to show that account's storage (how much is used, of how much). It asks only for the Google Drive scope drive.file, which covers files an app creates or the user opens with it. Velum creates and opens none, so it can't see the user's files. It calls Drive's about endpoint for the account's email address and storage quota, and nothing else.
